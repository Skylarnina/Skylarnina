# Kóda: reference DNA

This file records what each reference site gives us and what it doesn't. We take principles only, never layouts or brand looks. Every "take" has to fit Kóda's own rules: six colours, Cormorant and Inter, 0.5px lines, pills only for interactive things, no shadows, gradients or cards.

Studied from the user's full-page captures: Wispr Flow (9-page PDF), Ooshot, Squarespace, Otter and Adomate. These are static captures, so scroll behaviour has been inferred from the page layout and should be checked against the live sites before the enhanced build.

---

## Wispr Flow: *the scroll story and the italic phrase*

**What we saw**
- A warm, pale ground. Each serif headline has one italic phrase ("Don't type, *just speak.*", "4x faster *than typing*", "Built around *how you work,*", "Good *questions.*").
- A small tracked-caps eyebrow above the hero.
- Two curved text paths. One is a faint grey sentence looping on the left of the hero. The other is a heavy black ribbon of white text sweeping across it, with a waveform pill.
- Full-width panels with large top radii slide up over each other (dark, then deep green, then pale again).
- The "Speak naturally / Edits as you speak / Use it anywhere" section:
  - a side index on the left with a small vertical marker beside the active item, and the other items dimmed;
  - a pinned portrait image in the centre;
  - a serif caption plus one line of body on the right;
  - a wide pale S-curve band snaking behind the whole section.
- A feature list where only the active item ("100+ Languages") is full strength and the rest fade to about 35%.
- The closing panel is a full-bleed photo of a person in motion blur (warm light, face turned away) with a serif headline inside it.

| TAKE | How it becomes Kóda |
|---|---|
| One italic phrase per headline | Cormorant italic 400 on the last phrase of each display headline only: "…*start to finish.*", "Chosen for judgement, *not for looks.*", "Everyone *home.*". Body text never uses italic. *(Needs brand sign-off: F3.)* |
| Text on a curved path | "The route home": one **0.5px Stone line** with the evening's times set along it in Inter 500 at 9.5px, tracked. It's a hairline, not a ribbon, with no fill, no waveform and no pill. |
| The wide S-curve behind the story | The same route line continues between sections at 0.5px, never as a thick band. |
| Sticky scroll story (index / pinned image / caption) | Section 4, "One evening, handled". Times on the left, active one at 100% with a Stone marker, the others dimmed. A pinned portrait crossfades between five moments, with a Cormorant caption on the right. |
| Panels sliding up over the previous section | Section 5: a Navy panel slides over the evening story. Square corners in brand mode; a 28px top radius in enhanced mode only. |
| Dimmed feature list | Section 6, "Built around your evening": the line in the centre of the screen is at 100%, the rest at 38%. |
| Candid photo in motion blur, face turned away | Our photo direction: people in motion, backs turned, natural blur. This fits the brand book's "a figure in motion". |

| DO NOT TAKE | Why |
|---|---|
| Lavender buttons, the fingerprint widget, the black text ribbon, the waveform pill | Colours and widgets outside the brand; the ribbon breaks the 0.5px line rule |
| Logo marquee, testimonial cards, the compliance badges box | Banned patterns; there are no reviews yet |
| SaaS mock-ups (chat boxes, the keyboard vs. Flow comparison) | Product UI; Kóda has no app to show |
| Centred hero composition | "Everything centred" is banned; Kóda's hero sits bottom-left |
| Two-panel chat-style FAQ | We use Squarespace's hairline rows instead |
| Giant footer logo | The brand book says the wordmark "sits small" |

## Ooshot: *the cinematic frame and the caption that names it*

**What we saw**
- A full-bleed photo hero, about 520px tall on desktop, with one small white caption in the bottom-right naming the image ("Stellantis").
- Dot pagination on the white ground just below the hero, not over it.
- A two-column statement: three short bold lines on the left ("Produce locally. / Deploy globally. / Structure for the long term.") and three calm short paragraphs on the right, top-aligned.
- A 3×2 grid of square photo tiles, each with a white label in the bottom-left corner over a slightly darkened photo.

| TAKE | How it becomes Kóda |
|---|---|
| Full-bleed film hero with a corner caption | Hero film (dusk) with the caption bottom-right in Inter 300 at 11px: "Passeig de Gràcia · 20:40". |
| Dots below the hero, not over it | 0.5px Stone rings, with the active dot filled. Only if we use more than one clip. |
| Statement block: three lines left, calm paragraph right | Section 2: "Tell us when. / We arrange who. / *Everyone gets home.*" in Cormorant 300, with one Inter 300 paragraph on the right, top-aligned. |
| Photo tiles with a bottom-left label | Section 3: Day / Night / Weekend tiles. The label sits on a flat **navy 60% block** (the brand book's overlay rule), not on a darkened photo. |

| DO NOT TAKE | Why |
|---|---|
| Client-logo slider on a bone band | Logo strips are banned |
| Heavy bold sans headlines | Kóda's display face is Cormorant 300 |
| Centred section title ("Clients") and a symmetric 3×2 grid | Too templated; our tiles are a single row of three tall portraits (a swipe row on mobile) |
| Social icons, the subscribe box, the language switch | No icons; out of scope |

## Squarespace: *pacing, and the dark bookends*

**What we saw**
- A dark cinematic hero with film (a dancer in low light), with the headline and a small white pill on top of it.
- Every section below makes one point.
- The FAQ is three full-width rows separated by hairlines, with a "+" at the far right. There's nothing else in it.
- The close is a particle sphere on black with the headline and pill centred inside it.
- The page opens dark, goes light in the middle and returns to dark at the end.

| TAKE | How it becomes Kóda |
|---|---|
| Film hero, dark and confident | The hero film is graded cool and mostly shadow (per the brand book), with the copy sitting in the navy 60% bottom third. |
| One idea per section | Each of our 11 sections has one job, and no two consecutive sections share a layout. |
| Hairline FAQ rows with a "+" | Section 8: Cormorant 400 at 24px for the question, "+" / "−" as Inter 300 text characters, 0.5px Stone rules. |
| The particle sphere at the close | Section 10: a sparse field of 60 or fewer bone dots, like Barcelona seen from Montjuïc. Static in brand mode, drifting almost imperceptibly in enhanced mode. |
| Dark, light, dark again | Our evening arc goes the other way: golden hour, then dusk, then night. The page starts light and ends on Navy. |

| DO NOT TAKE | Why |
|---|---|
| Stats banner (14M+, $36B+) | Stat counters are banned |
| Tabbed product carousels, UI screenshots, the 3D template tunnel | Product UI; 3D is out of scope |
| "Trusted by 14 million" avatar row | "Trusted by" bars are banned |
| Gradient fades between black and white | No gradients. Our background changes are flat colour at every point (see F1). |

## Otter: *warmth and plain speech*

**What we saw**
- A warm off-white ground.
- A big framed hero panel inset from the page edges, with the headline and a small pill inside it and candid photos cropped into blob shapes.
- A kind, plain voice ("We like to keep it simple.", "We want to help you grow").
- Rounded cards, numbered circle icons, line illustrations, and an "As featured in" band.

| TAKE | How it becomes Kóda |
|---|---|
| Plain, kind voice | Short first-person sentences, as if spoken by someone who has done this before. "We need two days to choose the right person." No marketing adjectives. |
| Candid human warmth | Hands, backs, people walking away, a doorway left lit. Warmth comes from light and gesture, never from faces (brand book: no faces, no posed portraits). |
| A big framed panel with the headline inside | Our hero stays full-bleed film (per the section spec). The framed-panel idea goes to Section 5: the Navy panel is inset 16px (mobile) or 24px (desktop) from the viewport edges, so it reads as a panel sliding over the page rather than as another section. *(See F14.)* |

| DO NOT TAKE | Why |
|---|---|
| Blob-shaped photo crops, peach and mint shapes | Shapes outside the brand; photos are always full-bleed rectangles |
| Line illustrations | No illustrations |
| Numbered circle bubbles, rounded cards | No icons, no cards |
| Rounded display font | Cormorant only |
| "As featured in" band | Logo strips are banned |

## Adomate: *short-line serif breaks and section labels*

**What we saw**
- Serif headlines broken over short lines ("Turn data into / winning ads. / At scale.").
- Tiny tracked-caps labels above every section ("THE PLATFORM", "DATA AND SIGNALS", "TRANSPARENCY").
- Feature lists as hairline-separated rows (a bold title plus one line of grey body).
- Text and image sections alternating left and right.
- Neon lime bands, an iridescent swirl, a logo strip, star ratings and rounded product cards.

| TAKE | How it becomes Kóda |
|---|---|
| Headlines broken deliberately over short lines | Line breaks are set by hand in the markup (`<br>` at meaning boundaries, not wherever the text wraps), on desktop only; mobile uses `text-wrap: balance`. |
| Tracked-caps eyebrow above each section | Inter 500 at 9.5px, tracked 0.18em, paired with the section index (01–10): "03 · THREE WAYS". |
| Hairline-separated feature rows (title plus one line) | The structure of Section 6 and the Who a Kóda is rows. |

| DO NOT TAKE | Why |
|---|---|
| Neon lime, the iridescent gradient swirl | Colour and gradient bans |
| Logo strip, star ratings, testimonial cards | Banned patterns; no real reviews |
| Product UI screenshots, rounded shadowed cards | No cards, no shadows |
| Bullet dots on list items | No icons; hairlines do the grouping |

## What the five share, and what Kóda keeps from that

1. **One confident serif, one quiet sans.** Wispr and Adomate prove a serif display can carry a modern page. Ours is Cormorant 300 against Inter 300.
2. **A caption on every image.** Ooshot names the photo. We name the place and the time, which also carries the one-evening story.
3. **One idea per section, with generous space.** Squarespace and Ooshot. The brand book agrees: "when a screen feels empty, that is the brand working."
4. **Motion that explains rather than decorates.** Wispr's sticky story shows a sequence. Our evening story is the only place motion carries meaning; everywhere else it stays quiet.
5. **Warmth comes from light and voice, not colour.** Otter is warm in its words and its photos, not in its palette. Kóda stays in its six colours and gets its warmth from the photography and the copy.
