02What a Kóda is

A Kóda comes from Barcelona's front-of-house world: hotel desks, restaurant floors, guiding, events. They know the rooms, the staff, and the way home.

For an evening, a day or a whole weekend, one of them stays with your group, so the plans hold and nobody has to keep count. We like to keep it simple: you tell us when, we choose who.
