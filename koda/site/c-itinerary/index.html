07

Answers

Before you ask.

Is a Kóda a guide, or something more?
More than a guide. A Kóda knows the city and stays with your group for the whole booking: reading the evening, handling the small things, making sure everyone gets home. They won’t recite the history of every façade unless you ask.
How much notice do you need?
At least 48 hours. If your plans are sooner, message us first and we’ll tell you honestly what’s possible.
How many people can one Kóda look after?
Up to six. For seven to twelve people we arrange two. For larger groups, message us.
What if the evening runs long?
Your Kóda can arrange more time on the night.
Where do you operate?
Within the municipality of Barcelona. If your plans take you further, message us before booking.
Which languages do Kódas speak?
English and Spanish.
Can we choose our Kóda?
We choose for you, based on your group and your plans. It’s the part we take most seriously.
