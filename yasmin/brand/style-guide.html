Grid, spacing and images

The layout rules the pages are built on. Values from r3.css; the six modules are listed in MODULE-MAP.md.

24-column grid · the text measure (columns 9–20) shaded

Columns
24 (Fluid Engine desktop grid). One column below 768px.
Gutter
16px (12px on mobile). Bento modules: 24px (16px on mobile).
Page margin
clamp(20px,3.4vw,48px) → 48px at desktop, 20px on phones. Max content width 1600px.
Section spacing
clamp(88px,9vw,144px) → 144px / 88px.
Breakpoints
≤1099px tablet, ≤767px mobile.
Top bar
68px high, 1px hairline beneath.
Corners · shadows
Radius 0 everywhere. No shadows.
Image ratios
Cover (photo) 16/7 · Project logo tiles 3/2 · Snippets slideshow 4/5 · Headshot 4/5 · Baseline plan 2/3 · Placeholder 16/10.
Drawings, screens, documents
Whole, never cropped, on #F4F3F0 with 24px padding inside the exported tile (48px on the Rooms 03/04 cover plates).
Photographs
May be cropped to the slot's ratio.
Captions
Every image: "FIG. nn — …", 11px caps, 62% ink, 10px below the image (captions above in a Strip).
Overlays
Photo covers: ink at 0.38. Discipline panels: brightness(0.55) = 45% black at rest, (0.7) on hover; filter .3s ease.
Motion
One fade: opacity 0→1, animation-range entry 0% entry 35%, only where supported and when reduced motion is off. Buttons: background-color .3s,color .3s. No parallax or scroll effects.
