# Background photo

Put the training photo here as **`allenamento.jpg`**. The test picks it up automatically, and without it the plain Charcoal background is shown.

- **Subject:** real Oltresoglia coaching if possible, such as a coach working with a client, a barbell or rack, hands on equipment. Avoid posed stock smiles.
- **Format:** portrait or square, at least 1600 px on the long side, JPEG quality ~75, under 300 KB.
- **Composition:** keep the subject in the upper-middle of the frame. The lower half ends up behind the answer cards and fades to Charcoal.
- **Colour:** anything works. The test shows it in black and white so Saffron stays the only accent.
- **Framing on phones:** if the important part gets cropped, change `CONFIG.backgroundFocus` in `index.html` (e.g. `'70% 20%'`).
- **Rights:** only use photos you own or have licensed for commercial use.
