Featured case study — 01 of 24

Halden Residences

Client
Halden Development Group
Year
2026
Scope
Brand system, sales website, CMS
Platform
Webflow
Timeline
9 weeks, then monthly

A 32-floor residential tower in a crowded market needed a sales site that felt as considered as the building — and that the sales team could update every morning.

Problem

Weeks 1–2

Halden’s sales site was a template bought during planning approval. Availability lived in a PDF emailed on Fridays, photography was renders from 2022, and every enquiry arrived as an unqualified “please send info”.

2 daysfor sales to change one unit’s status on the old site

Solution

Weeks 3–9

We rebuilt the identity around the building’s arches and wrote a site that sells floors, not features. Underneath sits a Webflow CMS with one collection per residence.

  1. A live availability table the sales team edits from their phones
  2. Enquiry forms that ask for budget, size and move-in date up front
  3. A photography brief shot on site across two days in March

Outcome

First 90 days

The site now does the first half of the sales conversation. Floors 20–32 were reserved before the show apartment opened.
